Opening…

Open an Instagram chat

Read an exported chat the way it looks in Instagram, with search and a date jump for years of messages.

  1. Tap Choose message files.
  2. Select every message_1.html, message_2.html … file from your export.
  3. Wait while they load. Big chats take a minute.

Files are read on this phone only and never uploaded. A copy is kept in this app so you can reopen the chat later.
